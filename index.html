02 · Open Research & Public GitHub

AI Safety & AI Control Research

Principal Investigator (Engineering) at PegBit Studio. All open-source reference implementations are published under Apache-2.0 at github.com/PegBitStudio and pegbitstudio.com/research.

Flagship Research Thesis
"Can an autonomous AI agent's actions be constrained during execution and independently verified afterwards?"

Our work pairs Part A — Bounded Autonomous Systems (limiting what an agent can touch at runtime via container/syscall isolation, secret stripping, and loop guardrails) with Part B — Verifiable AI Execution (proving what it did via tamper-evident SHA-256 hash chains and offline audit verifiers).
